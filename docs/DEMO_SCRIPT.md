# 3-MINUTE DEMO SCRIPT

## Scene 1: The Emergency (10 seconds)
Hospital creates urgent request: O+ Platelets, 2 units, CRITICAL, submitted at City Hospital.

## Scene 2: Intelligent Matching (15 seconds)
System identifies 7 potential resources. Shows top matches ranked by priority score (94/100). Blood Bank A at 2.8km with 4 available units scores highest.

## Scene 3: Why the Score Matters (15 seconds)
Explain: System considered availability (40%), distance (30%), urgency (20%), and inventory freshness (10%). We contact the BEST resource, not just the closest.

## Scene 4: Real Response (15 seconds)
Blood Bank A receives notification. Staff member clicks ACCEPT 2 UNITS. System updates: Reserved count increases, hospital dashboard refreshes in real-time.

## Scene 5: Complete Traceability (15 seconds)
Show timeline: Created 10:30 → Matching 10:30 → 7 resources 10:31 → Notifications 10:31 → Bank accepted 10:33 → Reserved 10:34 → Fulfilled 10:39. Complete audit trail.

## Scene 6: The Innovation (10 seconds)
LIFE-LINK is not a directory. It's an emergency coordination layer that converts critical requirements into prioritized, location-aware resource matching. While e-RaktKosh answers 'where is blood?', we answer 'who should we contact first, why, and what happened?'

**Key Message**: *"In emergencies, seconds matter. LIFE-LINK saves them."*
